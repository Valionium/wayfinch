Measurements · optional

Measure inside the frame, tube to tube: under the top tube, along the seat tube from the bottom bracket, and along the down tube from the bottom bracket. A geometry chart's figures are longer.

With the saddle at riding height, measure straight down from the saddle rails to the top of the rear tyre.

Where the roll will sit, between the drops or brake levers, leaving room for your hands.

Today's details

Wheel and tyre details For MTB, a labelled 2.2 inch width is 55.88 mm. Check casing with the tyre manufacturer. For Goodyear tyres made for Zipp, select the exact model in SRAM's calculator.

Use the labelled tyre widths and the wheel manufacturer's rim specifications. SRAM's guide takes one wheelset size and rim specification; if your front and rear rims differ, check them with the manufacturer separately.

Include fitted racks and accessories. Leave empty bags, bottle shells and cages out if you will add their weight separately in the weight section below.
Internal width is between the tyre bead seats. It is different from the outside width of the rim.

Maximum limits are needed only when you keep a pressure result here. Use the lower applicable tyre/rim maximum. Minimum limits are optional if the manufacturer does not specify one.

Use the strictest limits for the actual tyre, rim and tyre width. Hookless limits vary by wheel and width; a tube does not make an incompatible tyre suitable.
