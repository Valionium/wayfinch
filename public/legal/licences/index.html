opening_hours (LGPL-3.0-only)

The opening-hours library is used under the GNU Lesser General Public Licence, version 3 only (licence text). It is bundled into the app unchanged. Data

  • © OpenStreetMap contributors. Places, water points, routes and boundaries come from OpenStreetMap, under the Open Database Licence 1.0 (openstreetmap.org/copyright, licence text). The place packs the app downloads are served from wayfinch-data.pages.dev; the code that builds them is in the pipeline/ folder of the repository, and the bundled boundary file is made by tools/build-boundaries.mjs. Both are offered under the same licence.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): weather data under CC BY 4.0 (licence). Forecasts are made from national weather services' model data, which Open-Meteo lists on its site.
  • GeoNames (geonames.org): place names found by Open-Meteo's place search, under CC BY 4.0 (licence).
  • Copernicus DEM: contains modified Copernicus data. Heights from the elevation service (Open-Meteo) are based on the Copernicus DEM, provided by the European Union and ESA. It is credited on the elevation profile whenever heights were looked up.
  • Natural Earth (public domain, naturalearthdata.com): used only to check region codes; no shape of it is in the app.
  • The bag planner (Load Check) shows maps with Leaflet 1.9.4 (BSD 2-Clause, Volodymyr Agafonkin and CloudMade; served from this site with its licence file, /vendor/leaflet-1.9.4/LICENSE) and tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
