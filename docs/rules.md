# Rule register

One line per rule, in short words, ending in its ID. `npm run rules` reads this file; a test whose title starts with `[ID]` proves the rule. Section numbers are those of the design notes. IDs listed as `later:` in `tests/rules-in-force.txt` are planned and not yet proved by a test.

## Place pack format

- A tile written and read back gives the same places (place packs) [PIPE-FMT-01]
- Writing the same data twice gives the same bytes (place packs) [PIPE-FMT-02]
- Every place lies in exactly one tile (place packs) [PIPE-TILE-01]
- A tile never holds more than 5,000 places (place packs) [PIPE-TILE-02]

- Water class B is a spring marked drinkable or a refill venue (place packs) [PIPE-WATER-02]

- Water class D is never written to a pack (place packs) [PIPE-WATER-04]

- The drink-places setting adds or removes those places (place packs) [PIPE-KIND-05]

## Route and location

- The route line encoder matches the published polyline reference result (contract 5) [LOC-POLYLINE]
- The route hash is the SHA-256 of the line and the missing-heights list, and any height change alters it (§4.3, Contract 5) [LOC-HASH]
- A position projects onto the route with distance along it taken from the haversine totals (§6.1) [LOC-PROJECT]
- An out-and-back position is settled by the last position, else the rider is asked once (§6.1) [LOC-AMBIG]
- Off route means farther than 300 m or the accuracy, whichever is larger (§6.1) [LOC-OFFROUTE]

- A manually set position always wins over GPS and lies on the route (§6.1) [LOC-MANUAL]
- Places match the route within 1 km, once per pass, and close passes merge (§4.5, §6.0) [LOC-MATCH]

- Today's stop is the end of the stage the day start lies in, never beyond the route end (§6.2, §6.2a) [PACE-STOPRULE]
- A later day starts at the previous stop at the daily start time, or at a late arrival (§6.2, §6.2a) [PACE-LATER]

- Every caller gets the same unrounded arrival for the same place and departure (§6.2a) [PACE-ONE-TIME]

- A closed next day sets a leave-by to reach the last open shop (§6.12) [LB-CLOSURE]
- The tighter of daylight and shop closing binds (§6.12) [LB-LATEST-SHOP]
- Split opening hours use the closing before the break when it binds (§6.12) [LB-LUNCH]
- A shop off the route moves the limits by its detour (§6.12) [LB-DETOUR]
- Shops with unknown hours never set a leave-by (§6.12) [LB-UNKNOWN]
- The closure limit follows the holiday at the stop's zone (§6.12) [LB-HOLIDAY]
- The water plan can limit the leave-by only for sources before the stop (§6.12) [LB-WATER]
- When limits clash the earliest binds and the card names it (§6.12) [LB-CLASH]
- When no start can reach the stop in light the card says so (§6.12) [LB-NO-DAYLIGHT]
- On the departure date leave-by states fine, passed or arrival time (§6.12) [LB-DEPARTURE-DAY]
- On the last day the closure limit does not apply (§6.12) [LB-LAST-DAY]
- Leave-by status follows how soon and whether it was passed (§6.12) [LB-STATUS]
- A leave-by before first light is flagged (§6.12) [LB-FIRST-LIGHT]
- An approximate position is computed from the near edge (§6.12) [LB-APPROX]
- Food for tonight applies for camping and mixed stays when closure does not (§6.12) [LB-NIGHT]
- A shop just past the stop counts and one beyond 1 km does not (§6.2a, §6.12) [LB-AT-STOP]
- Leave-by lines use the fixed wording with rounded times (§6.12) [LB-WORDING]
- The leave-by basis names pace, breaks, start and exclusions (§6.12) [LB-BASIS]
- Missing date, places or heights degrade the answer honestly (§6.12, §8) [LB-DEGRADED]
- While riding and after the ride the card judges the right ride (§6.12) [LB-SINGLE-DAY-RIDE]
- On the route end day the card judges the end (§6.2a) [LB-ROUTE-END-DAY]
- The leave-by meets every limit and any later start breaks one (§6.12) [LB-PROP-MEETS]
- With no leave-by no start from 04:00 meets daylight (§6.12) [LB-PROP-NONE]
- Unknown hours never set a leave-by (§6.12) [LB-PROP-UNKNOWN]
- Water sources in the leave-by match the Water card's (§6.12) [LB-PROP-S1S2]
- The leave-by properties hold on all six fixture routes (§1, §11) [LB-FIXTURES]
