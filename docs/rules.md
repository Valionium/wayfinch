# Rule register

One line per rule, in short words, ending in its ID. `npm run rules` reads this file; a test whose title starts with `[ID]` proves the rule. Section numbers are those of the design notes. IDs listed as `later:` in `tests/rules-in-force.txt` are planned and not yet proved by a test.

## Place pack format

- A tile written and read back gives the same places (place packs) [PIPE-FMT-01]
- Writing the same data twice gives the same bytes (place packs) [PIPE-FMT-02]
- Every place lies in exactly one tile (place packs) [PIPE-TILE-01]
- A tile never holds more than 5,000 places (place packs) [PIPE-TILE-02]

## Place pack classification

- Water class A is a certified or plainly mapped drinking point (place packs) [PIPE-WATER-01]
- Water class B is a spring marked drinkable or a refill venue (place packs) [PIPE-WATER-02]
- Water class C is an unmarked spring or a cemetery tap (place packs) [PIPE-WATER-03]
- Water class D is never written to a pack (place packs) [PIPE-WATER-04]
- Every object gets exactly one kind (place packs) [PIPE-KIND-01]
- A fuel shop is told apart from a plain fuel station (place packs) [PIPE-KIND-02]
- Stations are told apart from halts (place packs) [PIPE-KIND-03]
- Only whitelisted tags and clean text reach a pack (place packs) [PIPE-KIND-04]
- The drink-places setting adds or removes those places (place packs) [PIPE-KIND-05]

## Route and location

- The route line encoder matches the published polyline reference result (contract 5) [LOC-POLYLINE]
- The route hash is the SHA-256 of the line and the missing-heights list, and any height change alters it (§4.3, Contract 5) [LOC-HASH]
- A position projects onto the route with distance along it taken from the haversine totals (§6.1) [LOC-PROJECT]
- An out-and-back position is settled by the last position, else the rider is asked once (§6.1) [LOC-AMBIG]
- Off route means farther than 300 m or the accuracy, whichever is larger (§6.1) [LOC-OFFROUTE]
- More than 5 km from the route sets the far-away flag (§6.1) [LOC-FAR]

- A manually set position always wins over GPS and lies on the route (§6.1) [LOC-MANUAL]
- Places match the route within 1 km, once per pass, and close passes merge (§4.5, §6.0) [LOC-MATCH]

## Pace

- Riding time and clock time for a flat stretch follow the pace function (§6.2, §6.12) [PACE-RIDING]
- Loaded speed is 82 percent of flat speed and climbs are slower than flats (§6.2, §7.1) [PACE-EFFORT]
- A detour adds its one-way time at the place and its round trip to everything later (§6.2, §6.12) [PACE-DETOUR]
- Rejoining the route from a camp off the route is a flat leg at loaded pace (§6.2) [PACE-REJOIN]
- A place behind the rider costs the ride back plus the detour (§6.2) [PACE-BEHIND]
- Today's stop is the end of the stage the day start lies in, never beyond the route end (§6.2, §6.2a) [PACE-STOPRULE]
- A later day starts at the previous stop at the daily start time, or at a late arrival (§6.2, §6.2a) [PACE-LATER]
- The pace correction from check-ins is a ratio kept between 0.85 and 1.5 (§6.2) [PACE-R]
- The pace correction scales the rest of today but not tomorrow's ride from its departure (§6.2, §6.2a) [PACE-R-LATER]
- Every caller gets the same unrounded arrival for the same place and departure (§6.2a) [PACE-ONE-TIME]
